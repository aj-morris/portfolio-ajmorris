About Me

I’m AJ Morris, a junior at Texas Christian University studying Strategic Communication and a minor in Digital Culture & Data Analytics. Originally from Los Angeles, California, I’ve always been drawn to the way storytelling, sports, media, and strong ideas can bring people together.

At TCU, I’ve been able to explore those interests through leadership, creative projects, campus involvement, and professional experience. I enjoy learning by doing, whether that means building a website, creating content, working with organizations, or taking on a new leadership challenge.

Looking ahead, I hope to build a career in sports media, brand strategy, or digital storytelling while continuing to grow as a communicator and creative thinker.

Experience & Leadership

Through my experiences at TCU, I’ve found opportunities to lead, build relationships, and bring creative ideas to life. Each role has helped me explore my interests while growing my communication and teamwork skills.

Phi Mu

Membership Director

As Membership Director, I help lead recruitment efforts through planning, communication, and relationship building. This role has taught me how to coordinate details, work with different personalities, and help others feel supported throughout a busy process.

University Tees

Campus Manager

I work with student organizations to bring their custom apparel ideas to life, from brainstorming designs to coordinating orders. Through outreach, client communication, and managing deadlines, I’ve learned how to build relationships and keep projects moving.

Frog Army

Student Involvement

Through Frog Army, I support TCU women’s basketball by tabling on campus, encouraging students to attend games, and assisting with giveaways. This role allows me to combine my interest in sports with student outreach and community engagement.

Legacy Frogs

Athlete Consultant

As an athlete consultant, I help identify potential brand partnerships and develop campaign ideas that reflect an athlete’s interests and personal brand. Through brand outreach and collaboration, I’m gaining experience connecting athletes with opportunities that support their goals.
